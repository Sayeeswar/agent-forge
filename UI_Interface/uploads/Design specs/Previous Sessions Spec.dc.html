Grouping, Twice Over

Each heading carries both facts: recency first, because that is how a student remembers a session, then the topic, because that is how they choose one. Two lines of hierarchy, one row of type.

Within a recency band, topics are ordered by how recently they were touched, not alphabetically. A “group by topic” toggle in the header inverts the pair for a student who thinks in subjects rather than in days.
